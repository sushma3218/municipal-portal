# User Guide

Welcome to MuniPortal! This system makes it easy to report civic issues in your neighborhood and track them until they are fixed.

## For Citizens

### 1. Registering an Account
1. Open the landing page and select the **Customer / Citizen** option.
2. Click **Register** at the bottom of the form.
3. Enter your Full Name, Phone Number, Email, and a Password.
4. Click **Register Account**. You will be taken straight to your dashboard!

### 2. Reporting an Issue
1. On your Dashboard, click the **New Complaint** button in the top right.
2. Select the **Category** of your issue (e.g., Pothole, Broken Streetlight).
3. Type in the **Location** (e.g., "Main Street near the Library").
4. Provide a detailed **Description** of the problem.
5. *(Optional)* Upload a photograph of the issue.
6. Click **Submit Grievance**. Your complaint is now in the system!

### 3. Tracking Your Complaint
- Your dashboard displays a list of all your complaints.
- You can see the current **Status** (e.g., Submitted, Assigned, In Progress).
- Click on any complaint to view its details.
- If a Field Staff member has been assigned to your issue, you can see their **Expected Visit Date** and **Contact Number**, as well as any messages they leave for you directly on the complaint details page!

### 4. Confirming Resolution
Once the corporation staff has fixed the issue, the status will change to **CITIZEN VERIFICATION**. 
1. Open the complaint.
2. Review the "After" photos provided by the staff (if available).
3. If you are satisfied with the repair, click **Confirm Resolution** to close the ticket. If it isn't fully fixed, click **Reject Resolution** to send it back.

## For Corporation Staff

### 1. Field Staff Workflow
1. Log in via the **Corporation / Faculty** portal using your credentials.
2. Your dashboard will show complaints currently assigned to you.
3. Click **View** on a newly assigned complaint.
4. Click **Start Work** when you begin addressing it.
5. Use the **Send Message to Citizen** panel to alert the citizen of your visit date and provide your contact information.
6. Once you fix the physical issue, click **Mark as Solved**.
7. *(Optional but recommended)* You can upload photo evidence using the **Submit Resolution Evidence** button before marking it solved.
8. If a complaint is open for more than 3 days, a red **Overdue Alert** will appear at the top of the complaint reminding you to follow up.

### 2. Administration Workflow
- **Dashboard Overviews:** Administrators and Department Heads can see real-time statistics (New, Active, Resolved, Overdue) on their dashboard.
- **Photos:** The dashboard tables contain small thumbnail previews of the uploaded photos so you can quickly gauge the severity of an issue without opening it.
- **Assigning Staff:** Department Heads can open a forwarded complaint and click **Assign to Field Staff** to dispatch a worker.
- **Verification:** Once Field Staff marks an issue as solved, the Department Head can click **Approve & Request Citizen Verif.** to notify the citizen.
