# NagarMitra User Guide

Welcome to NagarMitra, the Public Grievance Management System. This platform allows citizens to easily report local issues and helps municipal staff track and resolve them efficiently.

## Roles and Responsibilities

The system is designed with five primary roles, each serving a specific purpose in the lifecycle of a complaint.

### 1. Citizen
**Goal:** Report issues and confirm resolutions.
- **Submitting a Complaint:** Navigate to the dashboard and click "Submit Complaint". Fill out the location, category, and provide a description and a "before" photo.
- **Tracking:** View your submitted complaints on your dashboard.
- **Verification:** Once a field worker marks a task as completed and provides an "after" photo, it will appear in your dashboard for confirmation. You can either *Confirm Resolution* or *Reject* it to send it back.

### 2. Receiving Officer
**Goal:** Triage and forward complaints.
- **Dashboard:** You will see all newly submitted complaints.
- **Action:** Review the complaint details and click **Forward to Dept** if it is valid, or **Reject** if it's spam/invalid. 

### 3. Department Head
**Goal:** Manage staff assignments and monitor SLAs.
- **Assignment:** For complaints forwarded to your department, use the action panel to select a field worker from your team and click **Assign to Field Staff**.
- **Monitoring:** Track overdue complaints. If a task is open for more than 3 days, you can issue an urgent warning (which sends a "Meet our office" alert to the worker).
- **Verification:** After a field worker submits evidence, you must review the "after" photo. Click **Approve & Request Citizen Verif.** to forward it to the citizen, or **Reject & Rework** to send it back to the field worker.

### 4. Field Staff
**Goal:** Perform the physical work and update statuses.
- **Starting Work:** Open an assigned complaint and click **Start Work**.
- **Messaging:** Keep citizens informed by providing an *Expected Visit Date*, your *Contact Number*, and a brief *Message*.
- **Completion:** Once the work is done, take a photo of the completed work. Click **Submit Resolution Evidence** to upload the photo, and then click **Mark as Solved**.
- **Alerts:** Pay attention to any urgent warnings or overdue alerts at the top of a complaint page.

### 5. Administrator (Admin)
**Goal:** Full system oversight.
- The Admin can view all complaints across all departments and has the ability to perform actions on behalf of any other role if necessary.

## General Navigation
- **Top Navigation Bar:** Click the NagarMitra logo to return to your main dashboard at any time.
- **Audit Trail:** Every action taken on a complaint is recorded in the Audit Trail on the right side (or bottom on mobile) of the complaint details page.
